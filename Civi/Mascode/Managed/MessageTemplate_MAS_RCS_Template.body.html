These two forms (RCS and SAS) will give our Volunteer Consultants more information to help them decide if they would be right for your project.

Please note we post all available projects for our Volunteer Consultants on a weekly basis and it may take up to a month for a project to be picked up.  Once a Volunteer Consultant indicates interest we will refer them to you for a ‘meet n greet’ and to allow you both to determine if and how you may proceed with the work.  Please note our Volunteer Consultants provide analysis, advice and guidance but do not generally engage in hands on work.  Please don’t hesitate to call me if you would like further detail on any element of this process.

MAS does not charge for its services and receives no external funding. Our ability to help other non-profit organizations is made possible by donations from clients who have benefited from our work.
When your project is complete—and if you are happy with the results—we hope you will consider donating to MAS. Your support enables us to continue providing expert consulting to other non-profit organizations that otherwise could not afford it.
Organizations choose a donation amount that reflects both their budget and the value they received from the consulting. Every donation, regardless of size, is sincerely appreciated.

We ask if our Volunteer Consultants have travel or other expenses that the agency reimburse them on receipt.  Unfortunately as we have no staff resources we cannot and do not respond to RFPs. Typically MAS is working for organizations that cannot afford to engage paid consultants.
